## The headline

**BR is not the only one working. It is the only one with an N — and the arm that earns is BR+OCR confluence.** Per signal: confluence **1.0264 R** on n=607 against BR-only's **0.8879 R** on n=391, a **0.1385 R** spread on the same book, 55.7% win rate against 50.4%, and 24/25 months green against 21/25. **Confluence is the only arm with a real N that clears the 55% win-rate leg of the money gate** (55.70%); the whole book is 53.2%. OCR alone is **-0.3152 R** on n=16 with a 95% interval of +/-0.7316 — unreadable, not refuted. The 84% rule produced **3 signals in two years**, and the constant the vault blames for that (`STRONG_PA_MULT = 1.5`) **is not on its code path at all**: the arming grade gate is, and it admits 5 of 434 eligible stop-outs.

### Confluence is not a fourth detector — it is a property of the other two

Every `[brocr]` row is also a `break_and_retest` or a `one_candle_rule` row; the label says an OCR was ALSO present at the level. So the sharp comparison is inside each base detector, where everything else is held fixed:

| | n | mean R | median R | win rate | months green /25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **BR WITH an OCR at the level** | 556 | 1.0583 | 0.776 | 55.6% | 24 |

| **OCR WITH a BR at the level** | 51 | 0.6787 | 0.549 | 56.9% | 15 |
| OCR with no BR | 16 | -0.3152 | -1.000 | 20.0% | 4 |

Break-and-retest **with** an OCR beats break-and-retest **without** one by **0.1704 R per signal** (1.0583 vs 0.8879), crosses the 55% win-rate line (55.6% vs 50.4%) and is green in 24 months against 21. Same detector, same book, same exit ladder — the only difference is whether an order block was sitting at the level.

**But it does not clear its own error bar.** The two sets are disjoint, so the instrument is the difference of two independent means, not the overlap of two single-arm CIs: **0.1704 +/- 0.3040 R**. That interval contains zero. Every read points the same way — mean, median, win rate, months green — and on this book the break-and-retest spread is **pointed, not proven**.

The same split inside the ONE CANDLE RULE is larger and does clear: **0.9939 +/- 0.8842 R** (0.6787 with a BR present, -0.3152 without). Read it with two cautions — the without-arm is n=16, and this is one of four comparisons in this lane, so a single marginal pass at 95% is about what chance supplies. It is a flag, not a finding.

## C. Is the 84% rule firing at all, and what is starving it

### It is not `STRONG_PA_MULT`. That constant is not on the path.

`signal_runner.py:91`:

```python
STRONG_PA_MULT = 1.5  # reclaim body vs avg body of prior 10 candles (84% rule gate)
```

The trailing comment is false for the shipped build. Both emission sites read:

```python
signal_runner.py:2240   and (RULE84_LESSON or self._strong_pa(current))):   # long
signal_runner.py:2458   and (RULE84_LESSON or self._strong_pa(current))):   # short
```

and `RULE84_LESSON = True` at `signal_runner.py:104` — a hardcoded module constant, no env var, no flag, no A/B. Python short-circuits `or`, so **`_strong_pa` is never called on the 84% path and `STRONG_PA_MULT` is never read there.** The codebase already knew twice over: the B4 comment at `:171` says *"RULE84_LESSON=True (line 102) BYPASSES the strong-PA gate on 84%-rule re-entries"*, and the in-block comment at `:2263` labels its own neighbouring comment STALE for the same reason.

Its only live reader is `_aplus_stack` (`signal_runner.py:1436-1444`), which needs first-break + displacement + strong PA to grant **A+**. That matters here only indirectly, and the indirection is the whole story — see the next section.

**Four documents say it gates the 84% rule. One says it does not, and that one is right:**

| document | what it says | correct? |
|---|---|---|
| `signal_runner.py:91` (its own trailing comment) | *"(84% rule gate)"* | **no** |
| `research/hallucination-audit.md:49` | *"CRITICAL — gates 84% reclaim"* | **no** |
| `research/x10_open_questions.md:114` | *"`STRONG_PA_MULT = 1.5` which gates it"* | **no** |
| vault `.scratch/omen-6/qa-queue.md:140` (Q5) | *"`STRONG_PA_MULT = 1.5x` gates this rule and ... has no author"* | **half** — the no-author half is right |
| `research/parameter_catalog_draft.md:16` | *"only active when `RULE84_LESSON=False`"* | **yes** |

The catalog draft got there first and nothing downstream picked it up. This lane is the confirmation, with the funnel underneath it.

### What IS starving it: the arming grade gate in `backtest_week._arm_84`

Every input `_arm_84` reads is a column in the book, so the funnel is computable without a replay:

| stage | rows | why |
|---|---:|---|
| traded signals | 1017 | the book |
| full stop-outs (`out == loss`) | 474 | a scratch never arms it |
| on an arming setup (`RULE84_ARM_ON` = B&R, OCR) | 473 | FVG / flag losers do not arm |
| stop-out lands before 11:00 (`SESSION_END`) | 434 | no re-entry to take after 11 |
| **survives `RULE84_STRICT` — `t.grade in ("A+","A")`** | **5** | **this is the gate** |
| (counterfactual) survives `RULE84_ARM_SGRADE` — `sgrade == "S"` | 39 | P7/G1's third arm |

The legacy grade on those 473 arming stop-outs: **A 7, B 466**. `_calibration_grade` floors the first with-trend signal of the day to **B** (G4: 968 of 1,016 traded rows are B for exactly that reason), and `RULE84_STRICT` demands **A+ or A**. **The grade gate kills 98.8% of everything that reaches it** — 429 of 434.

**Reconciliation with P7/G1**, which ran the whole 500-session replay instead of reading the book: P7's strict funnel is `473 -> 472 -> 7 -> 5 -> 3` and it applies the grade gate BEFORE the 11:00 clock (7 pass the grade, 5 of those are in session). This table applies the clock first (434 in session, 5 of those pass the grade). **Both land on 5, and both land on 3 emitted signals.** The one-row gaps at the top (474 vs 473 losses, 473 vs 472 on an arming setup) are P7's `counted` flag, which the book does not carry as a column; they do not propagate.

This is the two-ladder bug in its purest form. The rulebook sentence behind `RULE84_STRICT` is Austin's — *"you need an A+ entry"* — and in **his** ladder A+ is what he now calls **S**. The code tests `_grade_pa`'s A+: a candle-shape scale that lands on **2 of 1017** traded signals in two years (grade mix A 15, A+ 2, B 1000). One word, two ladders, a dead rule.

### The reclaim census — what the detector produces with the arming gate opened

Each of the 434 in-session arming stop-outs, walked forward from its own exit bar to 11:00, testing the shipped emission block (`signal_runner.py:2237-2255` long / :2455-2473 short) bar by bar: reclaim of the original entry price on a same-direction close, ORIGINAL stop and target, remaining reward >= 1.5x remaining risk, close not within 20% of the session extreme it is running into. `RULE84_MAX_ATTEMPTS` and the one-shot disarm are deliberately not modelled — this counts OPPORTUNITY, and the arming state machine is the row above.

So the detector is alive: **116 of 434** stop-outs (26.7%) offer it a bar it would take. The arming gate hands it **3**.

And the same census with the strong-PA gate **switched on** at each multiple — which is what `STRONG_PA_MULT` would cost if `RULE84_LESSON` were ever set False, and what removing it (0.00x) buys:

That is real but it is nowhere near the 98.8% the arming grade gate takes, and it is not applied. P7/G1 already ran the whole 500-session replay through the same funnel and got **473 -> 472 -> 7 -> 5 -> 3 signals** on the shipped arm and **521 -> 472 -> 472 -> 433 -> 116** on the loose arm (`research/p7_84_rule.md`, `research/p7_84_rule.py`) — a 39x swing produced entirely by the grade gate, with `STRONG_PA_MULT` untouched in both arms.

### And the constant has no author

`research/p11_parameter_provenance.py:37` files `STRONG_PA_MULT` as row **A5** with provenance `sr:90` — *the line of source it is defined on*. The constant cites itself. No transcript, no rulebook clause, no mark. Ballot **b01 q12–q15** records what Austin actually specified for this rule: re-entry at the PRICE you entered on, a candle CLOSE to reclaim it, at most two attempts, matching the trend direction. A body-size multiple appears in none of the four. `RULE84_LESSON=True` is, in effect, someone having already deleted it — silently, and without moving the comment.

## The three answers

### 1. Is BR genuinely the only one working, or does it just have the N?

It just has the N. Normalised per signal, **BR-only is the weaker of the two arms with a real sample**:

- **BR** — 0.8879 R/signal, n=391, 95% CI +/-0.2330, 35.7% of book R
- **OCR** — -0.3152 R/signal, n=16, 95% CI +/-0.7316, -0.5% of book R
- **BROCR** — 1.0264 R/signal, n=607, 95% CI +/-0.1837, 64.1% of book R
- **R84** — 2.0690 R/signal, n=3, 95% CI +/-3.0520, 0.6% of book R

**Delete every confluence row from the book and the mean R falls from 0.9551 (n=1017) to 0.8496 (n=410)**, the win rate from 53.2% to 49.4%, and months green from 23/25 to 21/25. The arm Austin reads as the sideshow is the better half of the arm he reads as the engine — though the 0.1704 R spread inside break-and-retest carries a +/-0.3040 interval and is therefore pointed, not proven.

### 2. Is the 84% rule firing at all?

**3 signals in two years, 3 traded — and 1 of the 3 landed on a day Austin graded S (TSLA 2026-05-19, +3.145R).** Firing, yes; measurable, no. The cause is NOT `STRONG_PA_MULT` — that constant is short-circuited off the path by `RULE84_LESSON=True` and cannot be starving anything. The cause is `RULE84_STRICT` demanding a legacy `A+`/`A` from a selector that issues `B` on 1000 of 1017 traded rows.

### 3. Is any of the four dead enough to DELETE?

| detector | verdict | the number behind it |
|---|---|---|
| **BR** | **KEEP** | 947 of 1017 traded signals, 96.3% of book R, 23/25 months green. Nothing else has the volume. |
| **BR+OCR confluence** | **KEEP — and it is the one to promote** | 1.0264 R/signal on n=607 vs BR-only's 0.8879 on n=391; 55.7% win rate vs 50.4%; 24/25 months green vs 21/25. Best per-signal arm in the book. |
| **OCR alone** (an OCR with no BR at the level) | **the only defensible delete on this book — and it buys almost nothing** | n=16 in two years, mean -0.3152, own 95% CI -1.0468 .. 0.4164 (contains zero, so "loses money" is NOT established). What IS established at 95% is that it is worse than the same detector WITH a BR present: **0.9939 +/- 0.8842 R**. Deleting it returns **5.043 R over two years**, moving the book mean by +0.0203 R against the 1.045R the money gate is short. Worth doing for clarity, not for money. |
| **84% re-entry** | **DO NOT DELETE — it is not dead, it is gated shut** | 3 signals in 500 sessions. The DETECTOR reaches a qualifying bar on **116 of 434** arming stop-outs (26.7%); the arming grade gate then admits 5 of 434. Deleting the codebase would delete a rule that has never once been allowed to run. |

**No arm passes the recall column, and the arm with the best money is the worst discriminator.** The held-out gate is: fire on >=90% of his S days. Beside it, the 42 days he refused.

| arm | held-out S days reached | held-out `none` days fired on | S rate minus `none` rate |

| OCR | 0/15 (0%) | 0/42 (0%) | +0.0 pts |
| BROCR | 2/15 (13%) | 9/42 (21%) | -8.1 pts |
| R84 * | 0/15 (0%) | 0/42 (0%) | +0.0 pts |
| **any arm** | **3/15 (20%)** | **12/42 (29%)** | **-8.6 pts** |


\* R84's two zeros are structural, not measured — the detection replay cannot arm it. Its real 2-year figures are 0/15 held out and 1/28 in-sample, from the book.

Every arm fires at least as often on a day he refused as on a day he graded S, and confluence — the arm that carries the money — is the most negative of the four (13% on S, 21% on `none`). **The detector that earns is not the detector that sees what he sees.** On 15 and 42 days these gaps are individually unresolvable; the sign being uniformly wrong across four arms is the part worth reading.

**What IS safely deletable, and it is not one of the four:** `STRONG_PA_MULT` on the 84% path. Unreachable code (`RULE84_LESSON=True` short-circuits it), no author (`p11` row A5, provenance `sr:90` = its own line number), and four documents currently blame it for a rule it does not gate. Delete the dead branch at `:2240` and `:2458`, delete the stale comment at `:2263`, fix the trailing comment at `:91`. Zero behaviour change — the selfcheck in this file asserts the short-circuit, so a regression would be caught.

**And the ticket that follows from this lane:** `RULE84_STRICT` is one English sentence read against the wrong ladder. G14 is already queued to A/B `_calibration_grade`, which is the thing issuing the `B` that starves it. These are the same bug seen from two ends.
