What is in this batch, and in what proportion

Sixty charts, shuffled. They are drawn to a stated quota, and which card is which is not on this page — the key is in the build record, not in front of you:

  • 20 mornings the engine booked a trade
  • 20 mornings the engine found setups and refused every one
  • 20 mornings the engine found nothing at all

That mix exists because the old builder never asked whether the engine took the signal it was showing you — 25 of the last 30 cards were signals it had refused, so every precision number off them measured the wrong thing. This one prices all three at once: do you agree with what it trades, is it right to refuse what it refuses, and what is it missing entirely.

Answer a card and this line starts counting the minutes you have given.
