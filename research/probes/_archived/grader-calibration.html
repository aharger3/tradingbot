Why these cards

Every one is a chart where the grader and you disagree. The first half are entries you took and it did not grade S. The second half are days you refused and it graded S anyway. Tuning on one direction alone moves recall and precision the same way and teaches nothing.

The thresholds behind those flags are numbers I invented. You gave the eight variables; nobody set the constants. Every flag you reject is one whose number is too tight, and that is the entire point of this deck.
