## Adversarial pass

A first cut of `limit_level` scanned its forward window starting AT the confirmation bar itself. The adversarial reviewer (2026-09-03) killed that result: the confirm bar's own low/high reflect price action that happened BEFORE that bar's close -- and the close is the only thing that makes the signal exist. Crediting a fill off that same bar's pre-close wick is the identical causal-impossibility class of lookahead this arm exists to exclude, just moved one bar later than the earlier-bar case it already guarded against. 96% of the first cut's 790 filled trades rode this bug (hand-verified against raw archive bars). **Fixed**: the scan now starts at `entry_idx + 1`, never the confirm bar itself. `next_open` was independently re-derived from the raw fills per the row's own required check and **confirmed** -- no lookahead found after a genuine attempt, hand-walked against raw bars. `chase_once`'s divergence from `next_open`'s trade count was also challenged: it rests entirely on `CHASE_PCT`, a constant borrowed from an unrelated tagging use elsewhere in `signal_runner.py`, not a defined 'how far Austin will chase' threshold -- **its specific numeric proximity to the vault's +0.028R figure is not a meaningful match**, just two numbers landing close by coincidence. The table above reflects the fix; the number originally published for `limit_level` was +0.7537R (793 filled, matching as_booked almost exactly) and is retracted.

## Verdict

**The vault's +0.72R -> +0.028R collapse partially reproduces, but not to the vault's magnitude.** as_booked (+0.7552R, 793 trades) and the corrected limit_level (+0.2760R, 659 trades, 21/25 green months) are NOT close: a properly non-lookahead resting-limit fill (order can only be placed once the signal exists, on a LATER bar than confirmation, cancelled if unfilled after 12 bars, and a touch at the exact bar extreme does not count) costs roughly 63% of the as-booked edge -- a real, large haircut, in the direction the vault's ceiling claims. But it lands at +0.2760R, not the vault's +0.028R: a meaningfully-positive, mostly-green-months book, not a dead one. as_booked's +0.7552R lands close to the vault's pre-rebuild +0.72R figure, so this reconstruction is tracking the same quantity the vault's older numbers describe.

**Austin's actual method (next_open, market at the signal bar's close) pays +0.2551R, $569/day, 22/25 green months -- essentially the SAME number as the corrected limit_level (+0.2760R).** That convergence is itself informative: a passive resting order and a market order sent at the close land in the same place, which is what you'd expect once limit_level is honestly scanning only bars AFTER confirmation -- the two arms are answering the same practical question (what do you get once you can no longer act on the confirm bar's own range) from different mechanisms.

**chase_once (+0.0564R, only 14/25 green months) is the worst-paying arm, and the adversarial pass found its apparent proximity to the vault's +0.028R figure is not a principled match** -- it rides on a borrowed, ungrounded constant (see Adversarial pass above). Treat it as "paying up costs real edge," not as "this is what the lost rebuild measured."

**Answering R1's question directly: the ceiling is directionally real but not to the magnitude the vault claims.** The naive back-dated fill (+0.7552R) overstates the edge; an honestly-obtainable fill, however it's realized (resting limit or market-at-close), pays roughly +0.26-0.28R -- a real, mostly-green-months edge, not the near-zero +0.03R the vault's headline number describes. `omen-blockers.md`'s specific +0.028R figure and 11/25 green-months claim do not reproduce from this repo's committed code at any of the four fill definitions tested; the closest arm to it (`chase_once`) is not a principled match. Austin's eye test and the code both being right is consistent with what this row found: the strategy is not dead, but the fill is not free either.

## What could not be reconstructed

`signal_runner.py` on this repo's `main` (998fbfec, 2026-08-24) has ONE committed fill model (`fill_price`, line 601) -- there is no `as_booked`/`limit_level`/`next_open`/`chase_once` switch at `:1456` or anywhere else, on `main` or on any other branch, local or remote. `OMEN-7.3.md` and `research/g80_lookahead_refute.md`, the sources `omen-blockers.md` cites for the +0.72R -> +0.028R collapse and the 85.2%/2.3% obtainability split, are not in this repo either -- consistent with `omen-blockers.md`'s own note that the 2026-08-30 rebuild happened on `Desktop/Projects/tradingbot/` and was never pushed. This script is a from-scratch reconstruction built only from what IS committed (the structural `level` every `fill_price()` call site already carries, and each signal's position in its day's candle sequence); it is NOT a rerun of the lost code, and the specific 85.2%/2.3% split is not something this script can confirm or deny -- only the CONCLUSION drawn from it (does a principled honest fill collapse the edge to ~0). It does not, at this definition. Run on blind 2R exit mechanics (`LADDER_MODE=None`), not the shipped ladder-B scale-out, so the exit and fill questions stay separate per the spec's own boundary; `reentry_84_rule` signals are excluded (see script docstring). Whoever revisits this should treat `as_booked` here as the reproducible stand-in for the vault's 'void back-dated fill' and `limit_level` as the reproducible stand-in for its 'honest' one -- not as a byte-for-byte replay of numbers that no longer exist anywhere runnable.
