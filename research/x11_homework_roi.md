## 2. PRICE IT — what 60 more cards buy on the gate

### 2.1 They add no held-out S days. None.

| | count |
|---|---:|
| held-out set (`probe_omen_test1_2026-08-27.jsonl`) | 100 cards — S 15 · A 27 · C 16 · X 42 |
| lane-2 cards that ARE held-out cards | **12** (all 12 are S) |
| lane-3 cards that are held-out cards | 0 |
| **NEW held-out S days the 60 add** | **0** |

This is structural, not bad luck. `build_h2_deck.build()` exempts lane 2 from
`marked_card_ids()` on purpose — the lane exists to buy the *level*, which the old grade
does not carry. Lane 3 asks no grade at all. Neither can create a symbol-day that is both
unseen and S.

And the exemption costs more than it looks:

| | |
|---|---:|
| symbol-days the no-repeat corpus already holds | 817 |
| **lane-2 cards on an already-judged day** | **30 / 30** |
| lane-3 cards on an already-judged day | 0 / 30 |

**Every lane-2 tap 1 is a re-grade of a day he has already graded.** 60 taps, 30 of which
re-ask an answered question. The new information in lane 2 is 30 level labels, at a
2-taps-each price.

### 2.2 The confidence interval barely moves, and that is not even the binding number

Recall today is **3 / 15 = 20.0%**, Wilson 95% **[7.0%, 45.2%]**, half-width **19.1 pp**.
Holding the observed rate fixed and adding S days:

| S days added | recall | Wilson 95% | half-width |
|---:|---:|---|---:|
| +0 (today) | 3/15 | [7.0%, 45.2%] | **19.1 pp** |
| +15 (one more 100-card test) | 6/30 | [9.5%, 37.3%] | 13.9 pp |
| +30 (two) | 9/45 | [10.9%, 33.8%] | 11.5 pp |
| +60 (four) | 15/75 | [12.5%, 30.4%] | 8.9 pp |
| +135 | 30/150 | [14.4%, 27.1%] | 6.4 pp |

A ±10 pp half-width needs **60 S days**; ±5 pp needs **245**. At test-1's measured yield
of **0.15 S per card**, ±5 pp is roughly **1,600 graded cards**. That road is closed.

**But the CI is the wrong instrument anyway.** Every recall A/B in this project scores
*the same S days* under two engine arms, so the honest test is the paired one — McNemar's
exact test, which is a sign test on the discordant days:

- A fix must recover **≥ 5** of the currently missed S days to clear p < 0.05
  (0.5⁵ = 0.031), **and that threshold does not move with the denominator at all.**
- With 12 missed days today, a fix that recovers each missed day with probability *q*
  clears the bar with:

| q | 12 missed (today) | 24 missed (100 more cards) |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.1 | 0.004 | 0.085 |
| 0.2 | 0.073 | 0.540 |
| **0.3** | **0.276** | **0.889** |
| 0.4 | 0.562 | 0.987 |
| 0.5 | 0.806 | 0.999 |

**So more S days buy POWER, not precision** — a genuinely-30%-effective fix goes from a
27.6% chance of being provable to an 88.9% chance. That is the real argument for more
grading, and **none of the outstanding 60 cards deliver it**, because none of them are
unseen days.

## 4. THE REPLACEMENT — one instrument, one tap, unseen days

### `omen-s-sweep` — "S here?"

**One chart. One question. Two chips.**

- **Card**: static SVG rendered in Python by `research/probe_chart.py`, 09:30–11:00,
  PDH/PDL · PMH/PML · ORH/ORL drawn, **no entry and no stop drawn** (a drawn entry is a
  leading question and lane 2 already proved the format works without one).
- **Tap 1**, always shown: `S HERE` / `NOTHING`. Two chips, 50% width each, thumb-sized.
- **Tap 2**, revealed only on `S HERE`, never auto-pressed: `AT THE CLOSE` /
  `AS IT FORMED`. This is the p25 field, asked as a chip instead of derived.
- **Nothing else.** No entry minute, no stop price, no setup, no prose box. Those are the
  five fields test-1 spent 272 of its 372 answered slots on, and `t70_test1_score.md`
  gates on none of them.

**Cards per page: 250, all unseen**, drawn from `research/w4_candidate_days.jsonl`
(198 nominations, already deduplicated against all 817 judged symbol-days by
`marked_card_ids()`) plus the 100 already-built, never-graded `omen-test-2` days
(zero overlap with test-1). Stratified exactly as test-1/test-2 are — `s_traded`,
`s_dropped`, `silent`, `low`, `offroster` — so the S rate is comparable.

**Taps per sitting**: 250 on a page where the modal answer is one tap. Test-1 returned
372 answered fields in one sitting and H2 lane 1 returned 59 single taps, so 250–320 taps
is inside demonstrated range.

**Seconds per card: NOT MEASURED, and that is a defect in every instrument shipped so
far.** `research/probe_page.py` writes no timestamp — no `Date.now()`, no elapsed field,
nothing. Nobody knows how long a card takes. **The sweep must stamp `ms_first_tap` and
`ms_card` into each exported row**, so the next ROI question is answered from data
instead of estimated. Design target: ≤8 s on a `NOTHING`, ≤12 s on an `S HERE`. At 8 s,
250 cards is ~35 minutes; at 15 s it is ~62 minutes. Both fit one sitting, and after the
first export the estimate is retired.

**The single number it moves**: the held-out S denominator, **15 → ~52** (250 cards at
test-1's measured 0.15 S/card = 37 more S days). Missed days go 12 → ~42, and by §2.2 a
fix that is genuinely 30% effective goes from a **27.6%** chance of being provable to
above **99%**. That is the number the project is stuck on, and it is the only number a
chart can move.

**Delivery contract**, unchanged and inherited whole from `probe_page.py` /
`probe_chart.py` — the shell is not forked:

- saves to localStorage on every tap, restores on load, visible saved indicator;
- Export → Copy all / Download `.jsonl`, no round trip;
- phone-shaped, chips ≥44 px, 390 px layout;
- **static SVG rendered in Python**, never canvas — nothing is marked with a pointer;
- **does not rely on the claude.ai artifact runtime to persist anything** (tried
  2026-08-22, nothing persisted; the page owns its own persistence).

### And a companion that costs him one message

`research/probes/qa-queue.html` already exists, has 13 questions each with a recommended
default pre-filled, and has never come back. Add the 20 unstated constants from
`p11_parameter_provenance.md` and items 2–8 above as `rule_ballot_batch03`. That is
~40 more picks, in the format that has already settled 48 rules for the price of a
message, and it includes the one constant W5 measured as the killer of 8 of his 12
held-out missed S days.

## 5. THE TRAP — what my instrument physically cannot record

`p25_midcandle_entry.md` found the last instrument could not record the thing he most
wanted measured: `build_omen_test1.py:696` writes `out.entry_p = closes[i]`, so every
S/A/C entry in test-1 reads 100% at-close and the field can never say otherwise.
**`research/probes/omen-test-2.html` carries the same line** — verified by grep — so
grading test-2 as built would reproduce the lie on 100 fresh days.

The sweep's own blind spots, stated before it is built:

| it cannot record | is that what is being measured? |
|---|---|
| **the bar the S sits on** — no entry minute, no entry price | **No.** The gate is *"does the engine fire at all on an S day"*, day-level (`t70_test1_score.md`, 3/15). Bar-level entry match is a secondary read at 4/58 = 7% and is explicitly not this instrument's job. |
| **the stop** | **No.** W3/W5 already named the killing gate from bars alone (`B&R_MIN_RISK`, 50 of 58 discards). His stop price adds nothing to that diagnosis. |
| **conviction / size / how good an S** | **No.** S is defined as zero downgrades — it is already a binary in his own ladder. |
| **anything after 11:00** | **No.** Ballot c7 settled *"after 11:00, manage-only."* |
| **why** | **No** — and deliberately. Test-1's `why` came back 43 of 100 times and no published number reads it. |

**The one field that could repeat the p25 bug is tap 2**, `AT THE CLOSE` / `AS IT FORMED`
— because H2 lane 2 already ships an auto-press (`level=na` is clicked for him when the
grade is X, `build_h2_deck.EXTRA_JS`). An auto-press on a measured question is exactly
the p25 failure mode in a different costume. **Hard rule for this build: tap 2 is never
auto-pressed, has no default, and a card with tap 1 = `S HERE` is not `done` until he
presses it himself.** The `--selfcheck` must assert that both chips are reachable and
that neither is pressed by any code path.
