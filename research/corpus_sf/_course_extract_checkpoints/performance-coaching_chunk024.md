I don't have, let's try this, I don't know how it works. No, it's only like my A plus and my A rate set up." (performance-coaching_session-39-q-a-community-review_transcript.txt)
- **Sizing - 100% for A+, 80% for A missing something** "My A plus set up size, that's my 100% percent. Right? That's my 100%. Oh, Neto, but that looks textbook. You don't double down on that?
