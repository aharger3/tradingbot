- **Valid retest/confirmation** "A read test or strong reaction on the key level. And you need to be very clear. You want weight rejection. You want train alignment with the indices. You want relative strength and relative weakness. You want a specific candle, like there's a lot of people like, oh, I only entry if I see a hammer. Or I only entry if I see an engulfing candle. I only enter if I see a green candle or a red candle or, you know, whatever. So you need to be very clear on what is confirming your setup." (performance-coaching_session-29-system-integrity-sticking-to-your-playbook_transcript.txt)
- **Entry trigger** "I'm pulling the trigger on rejection candle or momentum candle back into the trim. Or whatever, right? I mean, you need to identify what is your entry, right?" (performance-coaching_session-29-system-integrity-sticking-to-your-playbook_transcript.txt)
- **Stop placement** "I'm going to stop if I have a candle below the read test week or if the candle closes beyond my entry point or my invalidation level or the next level or whatever, right? - **Targets** "I'm usually taking 25% of my trade on high or low of the day. Then on the next key level... I take 50% of my trade on the next key level. ### 9. Psychology / performance
- **Rules over emotions** "your rules are way more important than your emotions, right? Because you can be overwhelmed with emotions. You can be overwhelmed with news. You can be all fog about a previous trade. Or, you know, like, you know, being overconfident or whatever. So your rules are isolating all of the actual facts, all of the data that is actually happening in front of you at your screen, at your chart. And, you know, isolating everything. So the decision is made on data and not on emotion." (performance-coaching_session-29-system-integrity-sticking-to-your-playbook_transcript.txt)
- **No revenge trading** "Never increase size after a loss. We don't want revenge trading. We don't want, you know, this is the trader nightmare. I need to make back this money that I just lost." (performance-coaching_session-29-system-integrity-sticking-to-your-playbook_transcript.txt)
- **No downsizing after loss** "Do you size down after big loss? No, I never size down... The question is not, how less should I be risking? The question is, are you ready to keep trading?" (performance-coaching_session-29-system-integrity-sticking-to-your-playbook_transcript.txt)

### 10. Concrete numbers

The reason is that it's, for me, it's harder to take too much losses and I'm okay taking a small win like today." (performance-coaching_session-29-system-integrity-sticking-to-your-playbook_transcript.txt)
- **Risk %** "I use a fixed amount instead of a fixed contract quantity... I usually take trades around the $5,000 to $7,000 trade." (performance-coaching_session-29-system-integrity-sticking-to-your-playbook_transcript.txt)

- **Sizing-up milestone** "I increase my percentage only when 20 consecutive trades log with 70% execution. Compliance. This is your milestone. This is your test.
