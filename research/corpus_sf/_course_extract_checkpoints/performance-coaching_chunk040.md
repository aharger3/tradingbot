### 8. Trade selection / grading
- **Core system stays stable; only expectations/management adapt** "Your core system must stay stable... What adapt is expectations, patience? How do you manage your trade, your trade management? And how selective you are with your setups" (performance-coaching_session-56-creative-thinking-for-trade-adaptation_transcript.txt)
- **Ask adaptive questions before every trade** "What environment am I trading? What does my setup usually look like here? What is different today? What adjustment improves my execution?" (performance-coaching_session-56-creative-thinking-for-trade-adaptation_transcript.txt)
- **Do not force trades ignoring context** "you are forcing trades because you, you are ignoring the context. You are watching a retest and you said like, oh, well, this touch here. ### 9. Psychology / performance
- **Discipline is not rigidity** "Discipline is consistency of process while remaining flexible in interpretation." (performance-coaching_session-56-creative-thinking-for-trade-adaptation_transcript.txt)
- **Detach from drawdown by pre-committing risk** "something that helped was when I started setting a stop loss, comfortable willing to lose for each trade. For example, if I raised 300 bucks, now enter each trade, assuming that money is already gone, that way you are detached from the drawback." (performance-coaching_session-56-creative-thinking-for-trade-adaptation_transcript.txt)
- **Visualize scenarios before market open** "every time the market is about to open, I'm visualizing the different scenarios in my mind... because I can be thinking without the pressure of... the stress of the market open, because I don't have money on the line" (performance-coaching_session-56-creative-thinking-for-trade-adaptation_transcript.txt)
