### 9. Psychology / performance
- **Emotional regulation** "Resilience means your execution, your execution quality stays stable even when your emotions fluctuate, all right? That's it. If your execution drops every time your emotions spike, you don't have a strategy problem. You have a resilience gap, all right?" (performance-coaching_session-53-advanced-resilience-training_transcript.txt)
- **Size reduction rule** "if emotional intensity, you know, like higher than six out of 10, you automatically reduce your size to just one quarter or even half your size. This is not weakness, guys. This is capital protection." (performance-coaching_session-53-advanced-resilience-training_transcript.txt)
- **Post-loss protocol** "When calm, ask only three questions. Did I follow my rules? Is this variance or error? And number three, what does data say? No emotion, right?" (performance-coaching_session-53-advanced-resilience-training_transcript.txt)
- **Impulse control** "If you can push this impulse for, I don't know, like three minutes, two minutes. If after two, three minutes, the setup still fits your plan, well, then take action. Right. Then take action. But the most of the impulsive trades, they die in the next two or three minutes." (performance-coaching_session-54-monthly-q-a-february-integration_transcript.txt)
- **Training to lose** "you need to be trained to lose, right? So when you start training your losses, you get used to them. So when you have another loss, it doesn't impact you like before, right? Because you have been already there." (performance-coaching_session-53-advanced-resilience-training_transcript.txt)
- **Execution-only focus** "For the rest of that session, you are not trading for money, all right? You are not trading for money. You are trading for execution score, right? For your journal, how I execute it." (performance-coaching_session-53-advanced-resilience-training_transcript.txt)
- **Consistency beats intensity** "consistency beats intensity. All right. So, we don't, I mean, we do want to, but we don't need a perfect day every day starting tomorrow. We start with consistent efforts and actions to get there." (performance-coaching_session-54-monthly-q-a-february-integration_transcript.txt)
- **Identity statement** "I am not my last trade. I am the consistency of my process, right?" (performance-coaching_session-53-advanced-resilience-training_transcript.txt)
