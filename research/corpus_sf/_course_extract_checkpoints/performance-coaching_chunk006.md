### 9. Psychology / performance
- **Confidence is built on preparation, execution, reflection** "confidence is not a feeling. It's a byproduct of how you prepare, how you execute, and how you reflect." (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Journal immediately after a loss** "when you take a loss, it's, it's important to journal right away. So you have your emotions, fresh, your, your reasoning, fresh, and everything." (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Journal immediately after a win with poor execution** "or even if you took a win, but your execution and your performance was awful, journal right away, identify what triggered your decisions, identify the emotions that you have in that moment." (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Don't compare results to others** "you can't compare your trades. If you're trading with $400, $500, $1,000, $5,000, you can compare your your results with one guy that just invested $50,000 on a trade" (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Don't copy others' setups** "trying to copy other setups with a different level of experience with a different level of risk management or risk tolerance, that can kill your confidence" (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Follow your rules even when it hurts** "follow your rules, even when it hurts, like today, if you took the NVIDIA trade, even if it hurts, you take your loss because that was your system, and you don't chase, and you don't revenge trade" (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Self-talk as a coach, not a critic** "speak to yourself like a coach, not a critic" (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Track wins and losses with honesty** "track wins and losses with honesty, right? Not only your wins, you don't only journal your wins, you journal your wins and your losers" (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Get reps for familiarity** "get reps in repetition, breathe familiarity, right? The more that you repeat the same thing, the more familiar you are with your setup. And familiarity breeds confidence" (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Overprepare** "overprepare, right? Know your levels, know your plans, know your thesis, know your different scenarios." (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Avoid chasing alerts without understanding** "chasing alerts or trades without understanding... following blindly to other trader, even if it's your coach, and you don't have a good understanding of the trade can just take you to tragedy, to losses" (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Avoid breaking your own rules** "breaking your own rule. So if you are consistent with your system, so you can plan your risk, and also you can plan your rewards, right? But if you keep breaking your rules, you are not letting your system to actually work." (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Avoid avoiding journaling due to emotional discomfort** "avoiding journaling because of because of your emotional discomfort... When you take a loss, and you don't want to journal, you are missing out on everything" (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Avoid revenge trading** "you don't revenge trade" (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
- **Avoid trading emotionally** "no journaling or training emotionally, right? Oh, I took a loss yesterday. So I need to, I need to, I need to make it back today." (performance-coaching_session-20-confidence-in-performance_transcript.txt)
