### Psychology / performance
- **Journaling (mandatory)** "if you are not journaling, I don't know, you are basically 90% of the education here or 90% of the lessons that the market is handling you" (performance-coaching_session-22-june-q-a-community-review_transcript.txt)
- **Detach from previous trades** "you cannot be trading with fear of loss from the previous trade, or you cannot be trading over confidence after a win in the morning. So, you need to detach from the outcome of the previous trades" (performance-coaching_session-22-june-q-a-community-review_transcript.txt)
- **Track behavior, not results** "I'm not tracking if I had a loss or if I had a weight today. I'm tracking if I was well prepared. I'm tracking if I enter my trade on my trigger." (performance-coaching_session-22-june-q-a-community-review_transcript.txt)
- **Stop attaching outcome to self-worth** "You need to stop attaching the outcome of your trades to your self-worth" (performance-coaching_session-22-june-q-a-community-review_transcript.txt)
- **Close charts if triggered** "if that is triggering you, close your charts, go to the kitchen, go to the restroom, walk away five minutes, then come back and reassess your session." (performance-coaching_session-22-june-q-a-community-review_transcript.txt)
- **Breathing exercise (box breathing)** "you pull some air for four seconds. You hold it for four seconds. Then you, you expel four seconds. And then you, you hold four seconds." (performance-coaching_session-22-june-q-a-community-review_transcript.txt)
- **Strategy must fit personality** "you need to find a strategy that fits your personality and not the other way around" (performance-coaching_session-22-june-q-a-community-review_transcript.txt)
- **Avoid too many charts** "You should not be looking at 15 charts or 12 charts or having a watch list of 10 different names. That's just too much, guys." (performance-coaching_session-22-june-q-a-community-review_transcript.txt)
- **Avoid comparing to others** "stop comparing to others, right?" (performance-coaching_session-22-june-q-a-community-review_transcript.txt)
- **PNL is lagging indicator** "Your PNL, it's a lagging indicator of progress" (performance-coaching_session-22-june-q-a-community-review_transcript.txt)
