### 9. Psychology / performance
- **Risk pre-definition** "pre-define your risk, not only knowing what your max loss per trade and per session is." (performance-coaching_session-23-overcoming-fear-of-loss_transcript.txt)
- **Sizing to let trades play out** "Instead of having a $2,000 trade, you have a $200 trade and you risk half percent of your position. Instead of 5%, you risk 50% of your position, you let your trade play out." (performance-coaching_session-23-overcoming-fear-of-loss_transcript.txt)
- **Emotions not driving decisions** "you still feel emotions... You just don't let the emotions drive your decisions." (performance-coaching_session-23-overcoming-fear-of-loss_transcript.txt)
- **Reframe losses** "A loss does not mean failure. It means feedback." (performance-coaching_session-23-overcoming-fear-of-loss_transcript.txt)
- **Celebrate good losses** "celebrate good losses... when you respected your stop loss, when you respected your invalidation level, so even the losses can be a huge win in your performance." (performance-coaching_session-23-overcoming-fear-of-loss_transcript.txt)
- **Process-based wins** "track your process-based wins, did I follow my plan, did I size properly, did I exit when I said I will?" (performance-coaching_session-23-overcoming-fear-of-loss_transcript.txt)
- **Journal fear moments** "review your fear moments, and journal, what exactly did I fear here?" (performance-coaching_session-23-overcoming-fear-of-loss_transcript.txt)
- **Self-talk before entry** "Use positive cell talk before entry, I'm safe, I'm prepared, I don't need to win, but I need to follow the process." (performance-coaching_session-23-overcoming-fear-of-loss_transcript.txt)
- **Breathing tool** "use the box breathing... this four by four by four by four box breathing." (performance-coaching_session-23-overcoming-fear-of-loss_transcript.txt)
- **Downsizing only as strategy** "if you are in a drawdown and you think that something externally is affecting you, yeah, it's super valid to start downsizing... but it's valid if that's the strategy...
