### 7. Exits
- **Scaling/breakeven/trailing** — "the less that you can care about being in profit and be a bit more ruthless about only caring when it gets to target, then it becomes a little bit easier to kind of stay in the trade." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Early exit as career risk** — "by getting out early, while you have money in your hand, you're basically significantly reducing your ability to be successful long-term." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Emotion masquerading as intuition** — "sometimes traders close positions early because they have the idea to close. It's like, oh, I feel like it's going to reverse. So let me close it out. And that feeling seems like intuition when it's actually just emotion." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)

### 8. Trade selection / grading
- **Post-loss process** — "make it part of standard process for the near term where you're going to examine the trade and it could just be a question of, all right, in what ways did the trade meet my criteria? If any mistakes were made, what specifically were they? If there are any motions present, what are they?" (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Handwriting requirement** — "I would actually force you to hand write it, right? Not like, you know, typing." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Stop after emotional trade** — "if you've taken a trade that you're unhappy with or subconsciously after it closes your, you can sense that you're unhappy, like alarm bells need to be going off, which says I'm off. So taking another trade is not going to correct your emotional state." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Intuition trades** — "play small ball, trade the system the way it's supposed to. And then over time, as you have a bigger account, you can start to actually segment out a portion of it in your own mind or maybe even creating a second account where it's just for those intuitions type trades you can research them separately." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)

### 9. Psychology / performance
- **Confidence from process, not results** — "your focus right now is far too much on the results. And as a result, right? You're trying to control the process in a way that is manufactured for results in a very artificial safe way that is actually more likely to lead to your failure in the future." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Reduce intraday results focus** — "you can still care about the results but relative to where you are now, we're trying to reduce it by like 80% on an intraday basis and make it more about caring about the results over a much larger period of time." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Patience from knowledge** — "knowledge of process is what underpins patience. You automatically become more patient when you understand how you make money at a very deep level." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Rule out emotion first** — "when we're dealing with FOMO, or like entering early, you wanna first rule out that there's not emotion present." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Overconfidence definition** — "you have to define what that means for you. Conceptually it is that kind of you curve where there's a certain amount of emotion we need to power the mind. But then if the emotions rise too high, that's where it starts to drop off." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Losing weeks are normal** — "there will be weeks where you lose money. If you're not able to tolerate that, then that may be the bigger part of the problem here." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Perfectionism worksheet** — "the perfectionism worksheet is gonna help you to affirm your accomplishments." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Trauma mental hand history** — "you're gonna go back through your personal history and look through particular memories where you feel like you failed, where the self-criticism was particularly harsh, and heal from that." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Double down on strategy short-term** — "I would go back and really double down on your strategy and understanding what you do well. And it's not about a kind of a fake it till you make a kind of thing, you're just gonna have to force yourself to believe and understand that this is how you're most likely to make money." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Perfection of process** — "the perfection in the process is what allows you to win football games... But the outcomes are not going to be measured on a game by game, individual person by basis here." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
- **Account buffer** — "the account has to be able to sustain a little bit of a drawdown that would be quite normal given your style." (psychology-coaching_psychology-coaching-session-6_transcript.txt)
