### 9. Psychology / Performance
- **Micro-habits over willpower** "Most traders try to win with willpower... Willpower, it's fragile. All right. It dies when you are tired, when you are bored, when you are frustrated. Or for example, when you have had two or three losses in a row." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Automatic behaviors** "Professionals don't rely on willpower. They rely on automatic behaviors." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Micro-habits remove decision-making** "The micro habits remove the decision making, no debate, no motivation required, just pure execution." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Light habits stick** "If a habit feels heavy, you won't repeat it. But if it's light, it sticks." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Four zones of mental game** "Every trading day touches these four sounds... The preparation... The execution... The recovery... And then the sound number four is about the reflection." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Preparation zone: no charts before micro-habit** "There are no charts before this micro habit, before setting you into execution mode." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Execution zone: interrupt behavior, not emotions** "They try to control emotions instead of interrupting behaviors... The micro habits create the space and space creates control." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Execution zone: three-second pause** "It's the three second pause before clicking by, by our cell." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Execution zone: read entry criteria silently** "Read your entry criteria silently before an entry." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Execution zone: one breath after order** "One breath after placing order or the if then rule." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Execution zone: if-then rule** "If my heart rate spikes, I step away two minutes." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Recovery zone: prevent revenge/overconfidence/carryover** "The recovery habits prevent revenge... Recovery habits prevent overconfidence... And the recovery habits prevent emotional carryover." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Recovery zone: stand up after closing** "Stand up immediately after closing the trade." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Recovery zone: write one emotion word** "Write one word describing the emotion... right after I close my trade, right there, like hot emotions, I write down what was happening." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Recovery zone: reenter with intention** "You take a break and then you can go back, but you need to refocus. You need to reenter with intention, not just reacting to what was happening." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Reflection zone: reinforcement, not just analysis** "Reflection is not analysis... it's reinforcement... What you reinforce, you repeat." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Reflection zone: highlight one good decision, not P&L** "Highlight one good decision, not P&L, not P&L related." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Reflection zone: close platform with intention** "Now you close your platform with intention." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Reset rule** "If you break one of your micro habits, you'll reset immediately. No shame, no spiral. Just a one minute break or five minute break." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Emotional check-in** "You do this emotional check-in and you decide if you can continue." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Yellow/red flag rule** "If it's a yellow flag and you just need more time, that's great. If it's a red flag because you took a big loss or your win was like very good and you feel like you are still rushing, well, you just take a break or close your session." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Five clean days goal** "The objective for this is five clean days... No focus on P&L, but focus on behavior consistency." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Reward after golden week** "If you can get the golden week with five clean days of following your micro habits, reward yourself, something small but intentional celebration." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
- **Habit stacking** "Once you created a habit, if you can stack another habit on top of it, it's not going to require all of the effort for the first one." (performance-coaching_session-51-elevating-your-mental-game-with-micro-habits_transcript.txt)
