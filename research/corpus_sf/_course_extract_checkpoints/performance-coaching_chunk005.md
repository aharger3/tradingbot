### 9. Psychology / Performance
- **Motivation vs. Drive** "Motivation is emotional. It comes and goes, and that's normal. The truth is you won't always feel like trading, journaling or managing risk. That's where drive steps in your deeper why your internal engine" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Drive definition** "drive is purpose base" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Drive loop** "drive takes you to action. Action takes you to progress. Progress takes you to believe and believe takes you to more drive" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Identity building** "Taking action, even without motivation, builds identity. And identity. I'm the kind of person who shows up every day. Fills long-term consistency" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Unrealistic expectations kill motivation** "Unrealistic expectations... if you have an inconsistent process and you have a poor win rate because in this, on discipline behavior, and you want to increase your win rate or you even want to grow your account, but you keep making the same mistakes. That's unrealistic expectations" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Lack of clarity kills motivation** "no plan, no preparation, no structure. That's lack of clarity" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Isolation kills motivation** "trading in isolation, no support or accountability... If you don't have an accountability body, my friend, you are missing out" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Comparing to others kills motivation** "comparing to others could kill your motivation like crazy. This is pure poison for your motivation... If you want to compare yourself to someone, the only comparison allowed is your journal from the previous days" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Chasing results kills motivation** "Chasing results, not progress... Only focusing in results, but not effort or growth" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Four trade types to identify** "our best winners... our worst winners... your best losers... your worst losers" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Best loser definition** "It's a trade where you did everything wrong, everything right, but probabilities just didn't work and you actually closed your trade where you were supposed to do it... that's progress" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Measure showing up, not feelings** "Don't measure how you feel. Measure how you show up" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Visualize daily** "Take a few minutes daily to reconnect with your life, especially before your session, before the market opens" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Track effort, not results** "your journal should say, did I show up and not did I win? ... Did I follow my plan? Did I follow my rules? Did I manage my risk?" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Rituals over feelings** "Rituals over feelings. Use your routines as anchors... The morning prep, the pre-trade breath work, the post-trade review, actions that anchor identity" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
- **Discipline with purpose** "Discipline without purpose becomes burnout. But discipline with a mission becomes unstoppable" (performance-coaching_session-19-motivation-drive_transcript.txt)
