- **FOMC day behavior** "From 9 o'clock till about 2.30, whenever the speaker starts talking or the Fed starts, you know, speaking, the market will go like this. It'll just chop around. That's all it's doing all day, right? Until the news event occurs. And then the second the news event occurs, the market will either spike up super hard or spike down" (the-accelerator-course_stocks_transcript.txt)
- **FOMC trade = gamble** "if you're taking the trade specifically for the move of FOMC, right, specifically for the move of FOMC, you're taking a gamble, right? That is a gamble. - **News days – avoid** "if I see that there's red folder news or there's big news events happening in the markets or, you know, there's, you know, a news event for a specific company or there's earnings... For me personally, I'm a very boring trader. I like to trade my strategy and call it a day" (the-accelerator-course_stocks_transcript.txt)
- **Earnings week behavior** "Usually what happens is the stock will just chop around before earnings. And it's because investors are waiting to see exactly what earnings will be.
