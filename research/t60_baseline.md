Generated by `research/t60_baseline.py`. Exit policy: **`30_30_30_10`**, the ladder settled in 5.2 T5 and re-measured after the ticket 02 stop fix.

**Assumptions:**

- Sizing: **1R = $1,000**, settled 2026-08-23 from Austin's own -1.25R = $1,250. The instrument is **options**, not shares — R-multiples carry across, dollars do not, so read the dollar column as an indication and the R column as the result.
- **SPY is excluded**, per `universe.INCLUDE_SPY_IN_BACKTEST = False` — and the corpus predates that flag, so it never contained SPY trades. SPY is 30 of Austin's 120 graded days, so every recall figure below ignores a quarter of his graded set. Ratification is **Q12**; flipping it requires a fresh engine run, not a re-run of this script.
- Annualised dollars project the corpus's own trade rate onto 252 trading days.

Win rate is measured **after** the ladder (Q4 default). Tranche 1 fills often, so this is structurally higher than a flat-exit win rate and the two are not comparable.

95% CI on mean R: **-0.0628 to +0.2202** (n=905, sd 2.172). Decisive means the whole interval sits above zero.

> **What changed on 2026-08-23.** The previous baseline read `+1.5148R` mean with a `0.648` win rate. Both were artifacts. `exit_lab` stopped trades out on **wicks**, against a rule Austin had settled five times, and then filled every break-even stop **exactly at entry**, so the runner could not lose and the distribution had no left tail. Ticket 17 made the stop trigger on the candle close and fill at that close, floored at Austin's stated `-1.25R`. The left tail now exists: worst trade `-1.25R`, **325 of 905 trades on the floor**, and the **median trade is `-0.9982R`** — more than half the book is a full stop-out. The 5.2 conclusion that *exits are solved* was solved by a bug.

The annualisation also assumes every engine trade is taken at full risk with no capital constraint, no commissions and no options spread — none of which is modelled anywhere in OMEN.

## Caveats that ride with every number here

- The break-even stop fills **exactly at entry**, so the runner cannot lose. That is a modelling artifact — mean R is a ceiling (ticket 02, Q9).
- Corpus B is the engine's own trade ledger. It is **in-sample**: the rules were fitted on these days. The only real holdout is the forward clock (ticket 13).
- Austin has only ever graded QQQ, SPY and TSLA, so every recall figure rests on **three symbols** — and SPY is excluded from the engine, leaving two.
