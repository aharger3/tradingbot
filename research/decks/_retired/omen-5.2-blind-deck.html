How to mark a card

Each chart is cut off at the bar the engine fired on (the yellow "fire" line). You are seeing exactly what it saw — nothing after.
take you would take this trade.
skip you would not.
Long you would BUY — you expect the price to go up.
Short you would SELL SHORT — you expect the price to go down.
The engine's own grade and direction are held out on purpose, so your call is not anchored to its call. Everything saves as you go.
