## 6. Defects

1. **The row was reported as landed with no number.** L3 exists to produce "OCR count and mean
   R before/after" and a gate verdict. The builder's `--stage build` subprocess died having
   written an empty log (`research/tape/logs/book_OCR_RETEST_DISPLACEMENT_off.log`, 0 bytes at
   20:07) and the report went out anyway. The number took 8 minutes to produce.
2. **The reported decision `"not_enough"` is wrong.** Measured, the row is a **hold**. Nothing
   about it was under the sample floor: the smallest gate cell is 380 trades over 12 months.
3. **The reported `h2_pass: false` is wrong.** H2 passes: −$111 → −$68/day, green 5 → 5, floor
   −$116.5. It was asserted with no measurement behind it. (`h1_pass: false` and
   `target_met: false` happen to be right, also unmeasured.)
4. **The loop ledger has no L3 row.** `research/tape/cycles.md` still ends at the corrected
   `RULE84_DECIDED` row and `research/tape/loop_state.json` still reads
   `cycle_count: 2, consecutive_holds: 2`. L3's hold is a third consecutive hold and is not
   counted, so O4's "stop after 5 consecutive holds" rule is under-counting. This referee
   deliberately did **not** write that row — appending to the ledger and pushing the ntfy line
   is the builder's `--stage gate` action, not the referee's — but it is owed. Running
   `python research/loop_cycle.py --config research/tape/loop.json --flag
   OCR_RETEST_DISPLACEMENT --on 1 --label "the one-candle-rule entry only when the retest
   candle is strong" --stage gate` against the two books committed here closes it; the books
   are already in place, so it takes seconds and rebuilds nothing.
5. **The audit's stated reason for dropping `clear_break` is thin** — see section 4. The
   conclusion (implement only strong PA) follows the spec's parenthetical and is accepted; the
   reasoning offered for it ("clauses the rulebook sentence never names") is contradicted by
   the sentence's own "after the break" and by three mark comments. The right follow-on row is
   a second flag adding `clear_break`, measured the same way — not a change to this one.
6. **`omen_bot.ocr_has_strong_pa` (`omen_bot.py:531`) duplicates `ocr_quality`'s `strong_pa`
   arithmetic verbatim** instead of calling `ocr_quality(candles, block, block_idx, break_idx,
   direction)["strong_pa"]`. Both call sites already have `block_idx` and `break_idx` in scope
   in `_ob`. The two agree today (4,000/4,000 in the probe above) and nothing stops them
   drifting — the same failure mode `CLAUDE.md` names for the stop fill ("never re-implement a
   fill locally"). One clause, two definitions.
7. **No test was added.** `research/test_t2_ocr.py` asserts `OCR_STRONG_PA_MULT ==
   STRONG_PA_MULT`; nothing asserts that `ocr_has_strong_pa` equals the clause it copies, and
   nothing asserts the flag is a no-op when off. The `book_id` equality proved the no-op for
   this build only; a test would prove it for the next one.

Pre-existing and not L3's: `cycles.md`'s `MIN_PT1_R` row is still priced on the full 28-symbol
pool while the `RULE84_DECIDED` row below it is core-11, so the two rows are not comparable —
already named by the L2 referee, still unfixed.

## 7. In plain English, for the push line

Making the one-candle-rule entry wait for a strong candle throws away 94 of every 100 of those
setups. The handful left over look better on their own, but there are only six of them in two
years on his eleven stocks — too few to call. Across the whole book it loses money on the first
year and only helps on the second, so it stays switched off.
