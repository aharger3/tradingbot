# T5 - the structural target

**Null result: the money gate is not an exit problem.** Across 47 target and scale-plan arms over 2,595 traded rows, not one reaches mean 2.0 R, and **0 arms beat the shipped exit by more than their own error bar**. The best causal arm books +0.5895 R (`hold_eod`) - a paired move of +0.0413 R against a +/-0.1576 R bar, so **inside its bar**, and it fails durability at 20/25 months green (worst month -47.92 R). 29 arms move outside their bar and **every one of them moves DOWN**. The shipped exit (+0.5482 R, 25/25 green) is already the best durable policy in the family.

**mean R = 2.0 needs +1.4518 R more than the shipped book**, and the perfect-hindsight ceiling on this book is +4.2392 R. The gap is not in where the target sits; it is in which trades are taken (section 5).

Generated by `research/t5_structural_target.py` from `bt2y_trades.json` (the shipped ratified book). 2,595 of 2,595 traded rows replayed; 0 had no archived bars. Entry, stop and side are fixed - only the exit varies. Causality proved by `--selftest` (truncated-tape replay, every arm); exit semantics asserted on hand-built bars by `research/test_t5_target.py`.

Reproduce: `OMEN_ARCHIVE_EXTRA=<extra data_archive root> python research/t5_structural_target.py`. Script and report land in _this commit_.

## 1. The arithmetic, before any table

With the ratified disaster stop (R1) every full loss books -1.000R, so `mean R = w*Tbar - (1-w)` and reaching 2.0R requires `Tbar = (3-w)/w`:

| win rate w | mean winner Tbar needed for mean R = 2.0 |
|---|---|
| 40.00% | +6.50 R |
| 43.06% | +5.97 R |
| 50.00% | +5.00 R |
| 60.00% | +4.00 R |
| 75.00% | +3.00 R |
| 100.00% | +2.00 R |

The shipped book wins 43.4% and its average winner is +2.563 R. **A flat 2R target cannot average 2.0R at any win rate below 100%** - that is the identity, not a property of this book. Austin's *"maybe we should shoot higher"* is arithmetically necessary, not optional.

**The ceiling.** Exiting every trade at its single best printed price before the stop would have ended it - pure hindsight, untradeable - returns **+4.2392 R** at 99.2%. That is the roof over every exit policy that could ever be written on this book. The best causal arm captures 13.9% of it, and 2.0R is 47.2% of it.

**And the book agrees with the identity.** Walking a single flat target from 1R to 8R trades `w` against `Tbar` almost exactly one for one - the product barely moves:

| flat target | win% | mean winner R | mean R |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1R | 68.9% | +1.000 | +0.3789 |
| 2R | 49.7% | +1.999 | +0.4904 |
| 3R | 37.6% | +2.987 | +0.4989 |
| 4R | 30.7% | +3.955 | +0.5206 |
| 6R | 23.5% | +5.562 | +0.5444 |
| 8R | 19.8% | +6.796 | +0.5432 |

That flatness is the whole result. Where the target sits is not what is wrong with this book.
