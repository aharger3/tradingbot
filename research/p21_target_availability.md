# P21 (W7) -- is there a 2R target at entry?

Generated by `research/p21_target_availability.py` over **1016** traded signals (2024-08-21 -> 2026-08-21) from `research/bt2y_trades.json`. Report only -- no gate, no routing change.

Austin, rule ballot batch 02 row b4 (`research/rule_ballot_batch02.jsonl`): *"its about sizing for the mean 2rr, so if there are no other levels to target, or its not an s trade to begin with, harder to trade."* G7 and P10 both closed on "the constraint is information at entry" -- this is the first candidate for that information.

## The level roster

The same roster `signal_runner.py` already assembles for the in-between-mesh veto (T11(c), `blocking_levels`) -- this script asks the mirror question, whether a level sits AT OR BEYOND 2R rather than between entry and it.

| level | source |
|---|---|
| PDH / PDL | previous trading day's RTH high/low (`backtest_2y.py::daily_ohlc`) |
| PMH / PML | premarket high/low for the session (`polygon_feed.py::premarket_hi_lo`) |
| OR high/low | first 5 RTH bars' high/low (`backtest_week.py`'s `orh`/`orl`) |
| HOD / LOD | session extreme as of the entry bar, causal (`backtest_week.py`'s `scale_level`) |
| T10 pivots | swing highs/lows, `signal_runner.py::pivot_levels`, `PIVOT_STRENGTH=2`, `PIVOT_LOOKBACK=30`, `as_of=entry_i` |

`has_2r_target` = at least one of the above sits at or beyond `entry +/- 2R` (long/short) as of the entry bar. 0/1016 signals had no usable level data (archive gap or <5 RTH bars) and are reported as a separate row, never folded into either side.

## Verdict

**The flag runs BACKWARDS from the hypothesis -- stated plainly, not dressed up.** Austin's rule predicts losers should lack a 2R target more often than winners. The book says the opposite: only 12.5% of losers had no target at entry, against 23.9% of winners (a 11.5-point gap the WRONG way round). No-target trades actually win *more* often (68.8% vs 49.9%) though for less when they do (mean +0.608R vs +1.037R with a target) -- reading consistent with 'no overhead level = open air, price runs clean to a modest target'; a real level beyond 2R looks like room but is also something for price to stall or reject at. **This is a negative result for the flag as specified**: it does not identify which of the 473 losers were unwinnable at entry, and by this measure a present target is if anything the worse sign, not the better one.
