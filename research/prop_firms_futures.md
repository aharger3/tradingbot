## What a Futures Arm of the Backtest Must Assume

- **Tick value**: ES = $50/tick (0.25 point), NQ = $20/tick (0.10 point), MES = $5/tick, MNQ = $2/tick.
- **Contract count for 0.3% risk**: At $50K, risking $150/trade, use 1 ES or 7–8 MES. Stops typically 4–6 ticks (ES) = $200–300 slippage acceptable; verify firm's fill assumptions.
- **Commissions**: Topstep/Apex/Alpha/Lucid/Tradeify use their platforms (commissions TBD per firm). MyFundedFutures charges ~$0.50/side micros, $2.50/side minis. Assume $1–2/contract round-trip as conservative baseline.
- **Binding rule**: EOD trailing drawdown (all firms listed support it). Positions must close by 4:00–4:59 PM ET; no overnight carry. For 09:30–11:00 window: all entry/exit within that window, stops trigger on close only (not wicks).
- **Pass gate**: Profit target / drawdown must be hit within minimum trading days (2–7 depending on firm). Most firms require 5+ profitable days post-evaluation before payouts begin.
